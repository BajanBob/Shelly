Security checklist

Shelly's encryption protects messages and calls on the way. These protect the phone itself, which no app can do alone.

  • Update your phone's system and browser as soon as updates arrive. Spyware like Pegasus relies on unpatched holes.
  • Restart your phone daily. Much phone spyware does not survive a restart.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Lockdown Mode. Android: turn on Advanced Protection where offered.
  • Fake cell towers (Stingrays): they can see that and where your phone is used, but not read Shelly. On Android: Settings → Network → SIMs → turn off Allow 2G. Prefer trusted Wi-Fi.
  • Don't open links or attachments from people you don't know, even in other apps.
  • Use a strong screen lock, and compare the safety code on your first call with each person.
